# Awesome Game Art Styles [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of game visual styles, organised by **look** rather than by asset type. Each style lists games that define it, the techniques behind it, where to learn it, and free assets that get you started.

Images link to each game's Steam page and are loaded from Steam; nothing is rehosted here.

There is also a [Claude Code skill](skills/game-art-style/SKILL.md) that uses this list to help you pick a style for your game.

## 3D: shading & form

### Low-poly

低多边形 · Few, visible facets; colour carried by flat gradients or vertex colours rather than textures.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/553420/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/553420/header.jpg" width="230" alt="TUNIC"></a> <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1562430/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1562430/header.jpg" width="230" alt="DREDGE"></a></p>

- **Games:** [TUNIC](https://store.steampowered.com/app/553420/), [DREDGE](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1562430/)
- **Techniques:** flat or gradient-atlas colours; faceted normals; strong fog for depth
- **Assets:** [KayKit](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/furniture-bits), [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets), [Quaternius](https://quaternius.itch.io), [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza)

### Toy diorama / tilt-shift

玩具微缩景 · The world as a tabletop model: soft light, shallow depth of field, everything small and touchable.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/2198150/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2198150/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Tiny Glade"></a> <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1291340/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1291340/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Townscaper"></a> <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/427820/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/427820/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Hitman GO"></a></p>

- **Games:** [Tiny Glade](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2198150/), [Townscaper](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1291340/), [Hitman GO](https://store.steampowered.com/app/427820/)
- **Techniques:** bokeh depth of field; ambient occlusion; rounded bevels; warm key light
- **Assets:** [KayKit](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/furniture-bits), [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets)

### Minimal geometric 3D

极简几何 3D · Pastel solids, orthographic or isometric cameras, architecture as puzzle.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1927720/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1927720/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Monument Valley"></a></p>

- **Games:** [Monument Valley](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1927720/)
- **Techniques:** orthographic camera; flat shading; pastel gradient sky

### Cel-shaded / toon

卡通渲染 · Two or three hard light bands, ink outlines, saturated colour.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1817230/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1817230/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Hi-Fi RUSH"></a> <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/49520/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/49520/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Borderlands 2"></a></p>

- **Games:** [Hi-Fi RUSH](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1817230/), [Borderlands 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/49520/)
- **Techniques:** stepped lighting ramp; inverted-hull or post-process outlines; rim light
- **Learn:** [Toon shader tutorial](https://roystan.net/articles/toon-shader/) (Roystan), [5 ways to draw an outline](https://ameye.dev/notes/rendering-outlines/) (Alexander Ameye)
- **Assets:** [KayKit](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/furniture-bits), [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets)

### Anime cel 2.5D

日式赛璐璐 2.5D · 3D characters that read as hand-drawn animation: authored normals, held frames, no interpolation.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1384160/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1384160/header.jpg" width="230" alt="GUILTY GEAR -STRIVE-"></a></p>

- **Games:** [GUILTY GEAR -STRIVE-](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1384160/)
- **Techniques:** hand-edited normals for clean shadows; limited animation (stepped keys); per-shot light direction
- **Learn:** [GuiltyGearXrd's Art Style: The X Factor Between 2D and 3D](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yhGjCzxJV3E) (GDC)

### Ligne claire / Moebius

清线 / 墨比斯 · Uniform black outlines and flat colour fields, like a European comic album.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/757310/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/757310/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Sable"></a> <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1931770/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1931770/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Chants of Sennaar"></a></p>

- **Games:** [Sable](https://store.steampowered.com/app/757310/), [Chants of Sennaar](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1931770/)
- **Techniques:** post-process outlines on everything; unlit flat colour; colour shifts by time of day
- **Learn:** [5 ways to draw an outline](https://ameye.dev/notes/rendering-outlines/) (Alexander Ameye)

### Painterly

油画感 · Visible brush strokes, painted textures and skies; light as a painter would place it.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/632470/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/632470/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Disco Elysium"></a> <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1172620/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1172620/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Sea of Thieves"></a></p>

- **Games:** [Disco Elysium](https://store.steampowered.com/app/632470/), [Sea of Thieves](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1172620/)
- **Techniques:** hand-painted textures; brush-stroke post filters; painted backdrops

### Flat-colour graphic 3D

平涂图形化 3D · Large flat colour shapes, graphic silhouettes, colour-coded atmosphere.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/383870/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/383870/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Firewatch"></a> <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/231200/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/231200/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Kentucky Route Zero"></a></p>

- **Games:** [Firewatch](https://store.steampowered.com/app/383870/), [Kentucky Route Zero](https://store.steampowered.com/app/231200/)
- **Techniques:** gradient fog by distance; flat unlit materials; strong colour scripts

### Retro-futurism

复古未来主义 · The future as an earlier decade imagined it: atomic-age optimism, art deco.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/377160/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/377160/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Fallout 4"></a> <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/8870/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/8870/header.jpg" width="230" alt="BioShock Infinite"></a></p>

混合媒介 · Different visual languages stacked in one game: 3D, pixel art, live-action, paper.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1092790/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1092790/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Inscryption"></a></p>

## 3D: retro & lo-fi

### PS1 / low-fi 3D

PS1 复古 3D · Wobbly vertices, warped textures, tiny resolution, dithered colour.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1262350/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1262350/header.jpg" width="230" alt="SIGNALIS"></a> <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/519860/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/519860/header.jpg" width="230" alt="DUSK"></a></p>

- **Games:** [SIGNALIS](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1262350/), [DUSK](https://store.steampowered.com/app/519860/)
- **Techniques:** vertex snapping; affine texture mapping; low-res render target; colour dithering
- **Learn:** [Godot Shaders](https://godotshaders.com)
- **Assets:** [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets)

### Pixelated 3D

像素化 3D · A 3D world rendered at low resolution so it reads as pixel art.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1055540/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1055540/header.jpg" width="230" alt="A Short Hike"></a></p>

- **Games:** [A Short Hike](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1055540/)
- **Techniques:** low-res render target, nearest upscale; pixel-snapped camera; edge highlights
- **Learn:** [t3ssel8r](https://www.youtube.com/@t3ssel8r) (pixel-art look in 3D)
- **Assets:** [KayKit](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/furniture-bits), [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets)

### Voxel

体素 · The world built from cubes; often destructible.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1167630/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1167630/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Teardown"></a></p>

- **Games:** [Teardown](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1167630/)
- **Techniques:** voxel meshing; small-cube detail on large forms

### 1-bit / dithered

1-bit 抖动 · Two colours and a dither pattern stand in for every tone.

<p><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/653530/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/653530/header.jpg" width="230" alt="Return of the Obra Dinn"></a> <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/913740/"><img src="https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/913740/header.jpg" width="230" alt="WORLD OF HORROR"></a></p>

- **Games:** [Return of the Obra Dinn](https://store.steampowered.com/app/653530/), [WORLD OF HORROR](https://store.steampowered.com/app/913740/)
- **Techniques:** ordered / blue-noise dithering; stable dither in screen space
- **Learn:** [Obra Dinn devlog: dithering](https://forums.tigsource.com/index.php?topic=40832.msg1363742#msg1363742) (Lucas Pope), [Acerola](https://www.youtube.com/@Acerola_t) (stylised rendering deep dives)

## Asset libraries

Free or cheap packs that work across several styles. Licences were checked on 2026-10-28; check again before you ship.

### 3D

- [KayKit Furniture Bits](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/furniture-bits) - CC0. 50+ furniture pieces on one gradient atlas, so you can recolour the whole set by editing one image. Also on [GitHub](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Furniture-Bits-1.0) as a Godot addon, alongside the other [KayKit packs](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets).
- [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets) - CC0. Huge library, including [Mini Characters](https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters) (animated) and the [Furniture Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit).
- [Quaternius](https://quaternius.itch.io) - Free. Pack pages say CC0, but the site now publishes its own Quaternius Asset Licence; check it.
- [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza) - CC0 and CC-BY mixed. 7000+ lowpoly models; check each one.
- [Synty POLYGON](https://syntystore.com/products/polygon-office-pack) - Paid. Large themed packs; the licence does not address web builds.

### 2D

- [LimeZu Modern Interiors](https://limezu.itch.io/moderninteriors) - Paid, credit required. Cosy pixel interiors.
- [2dPig Pixel Office](https://2dpig.itch.io/pixel-office) - CC0.
- [Open Peeps](https://www.openpeeps.com) - CC0. Hand-drawn mix-and-match people.
- [Humaaans](https://www.humaaans.com) - Free; licence wording varies between sources.

### Textures

- [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) - CC0. Paper, fabric, wood.
- [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) - CC0. HDRIs, textures, models.
