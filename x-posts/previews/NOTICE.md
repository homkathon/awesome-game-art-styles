# Original X media previews and attribution

The JPEG preview covers in this folder are extracted from the original X posts:
17 were derived from licensed MP4s in `../media/` and five are resized from
images attached to the original authors' posts. The creator, post ID, original
post URL, media source URL when applicable, and SHA-256 are listed in
[`../../tools/x_previews.json`](../../tools/x_previews.json) and
[`../../tools/x_media.json`](../../tools/x_media.json).

These third-party media previews are **excluded from the repository CC0
licence**. The creators retain copyright and their permission to reproduce
here does not automatically allow others to redistribute the media.
