# Creator video attribution and rights

The MP4 files in this folder are copies of videos from X posts whose authors
have granted the repository curator permission to reproduce them here.
Each file is named after its source post ID. Original post URLs, author handles,
original post text, and SHA-256 checksums are documented in
[`../../tools/x_media.json`](../../tools/x_media.json).

The creators retain copyright in their videos. **These MP4s are excluded from
this repository's CC0 dedication.** Reproduction here does not imply that
others have a general redistribution licence. To reuse a video elsewhere,
contact its creator or confirm the applicable permission.

The remaining original X posts in the gallery have no downloadable MP4 and
are embedded through the official X interface instead.
