## How to spot it

- Characters move with realistic weight and timing
- Flat-colour shapes without outlines, or thin ink lines
- Cinematic framing and cut-scenes

## Techniques

- rotoscoping from reference video
- flat polygon fills
- limited palettes

## Agent notes

Steps a coding agent (or you) can follow to reproduce the look. The [game-art-style skill](../../skills/game-art-style/SKILL.md) reads this section. When it runs, put a screenshot of your scene next to the gallery above and compare values first, then palette, then detail.

1. Film reference movement (yourself is fine) and trace key frames.
1. Fill shapes with flat colour from a small palette; skip outlines or keep them thin.
1. Hold frames longer than smooth animation would; the realism is in the timing, not the frame count.
