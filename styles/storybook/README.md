## How to spot it

- Soft painted or gouache-like colour with visible texture
- Characters drawn simply, scenes composed like illustrations
- Often framed by a book, page or paper

## Techniques

- layered 2D illustration with parallax
- paper and paint textures
- book or page framing

## Agent notes

Steps a coding agent (or you) can follow to reproduce the look. The [game-art-style skill](../../skills/game-art-style/SKILL.md) reads this section.

1. Paint backgrounds as illustrations in a few depth layers and move them with parallax.
1. Overlay a paper or gouache texture on everything, including UI.
1. Compose every screen like a book spread: a clear focal point and room for text.
