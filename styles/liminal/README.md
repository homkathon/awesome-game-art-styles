## How to spot it

- Real-world interiors with nobody in them
- Flat fluorescent light, tiles, carpet, repeating rooms
- Photoreal surfaces, often with a camcorder look

## Techniques

- photoreal materials on simple architecture
- repeating modular rooms
- camera grain, lens distortion, timestamp overlays

## Agent notes

Steps a coding agent (or you) can follow to reproduce the look. The [game-art-style skill](../../skills/game-art-style/SKILL.md) reads this section.

1. Model one modular room (corridor, pool hall, office) and repeat it with tiny variations.
1. Light it flat and slightly too bright with fluorescent panels; avoid dramatic shadows.
1. Use CC0 scanned materials (tiles, carpet, plaster) and add film grain and a slight lens distortion.
