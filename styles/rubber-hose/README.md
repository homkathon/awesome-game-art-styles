## How to spot it

- Limbs bend like hoses, no elbows
- Pie-cut eyes and white gloves
- Film grain, flicker and slightly faded colour

## Techniques

- hand-inked frames
- film grain and gate weave
- period colour grading

## Agent notes

Steps a coding agent (or you) can follow to reproduce the look. The [game-art-style skill](../../skills/game-art-style/SKILL.md) reads this section. When it runs, put a screenshot of your scene next to the gallery above and compare values first, then palette, then detail.

1. Animate on paper or in a raster tool frame by frame; keep line weight uneven.
1. Overlay film grain, dust, flicker and a little gate weave (frame jitter).
1. Grade toward faded sepia or two-strip colour.
