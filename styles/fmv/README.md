## How to spot it

- Real actors on video, not rendered characters
- Footage seen through a period screen, archive or editing desk
- Play is searching, sorting and watching

## Techniques

- green-screen or on-set filming
- short clips indexed by keyword or timecode
- screen-in-screen framing

## Agent notes

Steps a coding agent (or you) can follow to reproduce the look. The [game-art-style skill](../../skills/game-art-style/SKILL.md) reads this section.

1. Treat footage as data: cut it into short clips and tag each one (keywords, people, timecode).
1. Frame playback in a period device (CRT, laptop, editing bay) so compression artefacts read as intentional.
1. Use stock or self-shot footage with a clear licence; never ship clips from films or other games.
