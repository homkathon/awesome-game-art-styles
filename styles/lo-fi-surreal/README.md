## How to spot it

- Clashing colours and textures that look 'wrong' on purpose
- Dream logic: rooms and creatures that make no sense
- Rough, unpolished models or sprites

## Techniques

- clashing palettes
- photo and texture collage
- intentionally crude modelling

## Agent notes

Steps a coding agent (or you) can follow to reproduce the look. The [game-art-style skill](../../skills/game-art-style/SKILL.md) reads this section. When it runs, put a screenshot of your scene next to the gallery above and compare values first, then palette, then detail.

1. Pick rules to break on purpose (clashing hues, stretched textures, wrong scale) and break them consistently.
1. Collage textures from your own photos, scans and drawings.
1. Keep gameplay readable: one clean colour for interactive things, chaos everywhere else.
