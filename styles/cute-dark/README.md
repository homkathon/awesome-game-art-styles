## How to spot it

- Round shapes, big eyes, pastel or candy colours
- Subject matter is violent, sad or unsettling

## Techniques

- round shapes, big eyes
- pastel palette against dark subject matter

## Agent notes

Steps a coding agent (or you) can follow to reproduce the look. The [game-art-style skill](../../skills/game-art-style/SKILL.md) reads this section. When it runs, put a screenshot of your scene next to the gallery above and compare values first, then palette, then detail.

1. Design characters with round silhouettes and oversized heads and eyes.
1. Keep the palette soft; let the darkness come from content, lighting and framing.
1. Use contrast deliberately: cute animation on grim events.
