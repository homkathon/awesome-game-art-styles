## How to spot it

- Round animals lead the composition
- Matte pastel colour rather than glossy shading
- Just a few readable props in each vignette

## Techniques

- simple character silhouettes
- pastel painted textures
- three-plane scene staging

## Agent notes

Steps a coding agent (or you) can follow to reproduce the look. The [game-art-style skill](../../skills/game-art-style/SKILL.md) reads this section. When it runs, put a screenshot of your scene next to the gallery above and compare values first, then palette, then detail.

1. Draw tiny creatures with clear rounded silhouettes and minimal facial marks.
1. Compose a Node2D scene from a painted ground, two or three props and a quiet background; keep shadows subtle.
